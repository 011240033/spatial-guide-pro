Map a space

You can build a visual guide from panoramas without AR mapping, or try the experimental AR mapper. The panorama workflow is recommended if AR mapping is slow or unavailable.

Panorama-based guide

Use Guided sweep to capture spots in walking order, or load panorama photos. CLIP suggests room labels, which you can edit; DINO guesses links between uploaded photos. Check the arrows before relying on them. The finished guide is saved under Tours and uses camera arrows and spoken turn directions; it is not a measured floor map.

AR space mapping

This records a measured route while you walk and still needs Chrome on an ARCore-supported Android phone, Google Play Services for AR, and HTTPS. Add QR checkpoints at junctions for phones that cannot track the floor.

  1. Face a signStand in front of a room sign and tap Mark room. Type its number on the keypad.
  2. WalkMark room signs and add QR checkpoints at junctions; print each QR and place it there. Optional CLIP suggestions are processed after you finish, to avoid slowing the AR walk.
  3. FinishTap Finish to save. Start every later navigation by scanning one of these signs.
