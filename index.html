Map a space

Walk the corridors once with AR on. Spatial Guide records the paths, and the rooms you mark become destinations.

  1. Face a signStand in front of a room sign and tap Mark room. Type its number on the keypad.
  2. WalkGo along the corridor. Face each sign as you pass it and mark the room.
  3. FinishTap Finish to save. Start every later navigation by scanning one of these signs.
